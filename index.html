01 / About the society

Where students meet technology

The Society of ICT of Swarnamali Girls' College was established in 2024 to encourage students to explore information and communication technology beyond the classroom.

The society creates opportunities for students to learn, experiment, collaborate and develop practical technological skills through projects, workshops, competitions and ICT-related activities.
